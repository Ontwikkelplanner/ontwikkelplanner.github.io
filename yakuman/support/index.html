Contact

Questions, a bug, or a scoring result you think is wrong? E-mail appstore@ontwikkelplanner.nl. For scoring questions, please include the hand, the winning tile, and whether it was ron or tsumo.

Frequently asked questions

I bought the unlock, but the app does not show it.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Which rules does the app use?

By default the app follows the Tenhou four-player ranked rules. You can also choose Mahjong Soul or EMA (European) rules. The app is not affiliated with Tenhou, Mahjong Soul or the European Mahjong Association.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Playing, learning and the score helper all work offline.

Veelgestelde vragen

Ik heb de ontgrendeling gekocht, maar de app laat die niet zien.

Open Instellingen in de app en tik op Aankopen herstellen, ingelogd met het Apple-account waarmee je hebt gekocht.

Welke regels gebruikt de app?

Standaard volgt de app de regels van Tenhou (rangspel voor vier spelers). Je kunt ook kiezen voor de regels van Mahjong Soul of de EMA (Europees). De app is niet verbonden aan Tenhou, Mahjong Soul of de European Mahjong Association.

Heeft de app internet nodig?

Nee. Spelen, leren en de tafelhulp werken allemaal offline.
